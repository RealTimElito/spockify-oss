Proposed roadmap

Strategic direction for a self-hosted coding stack — IDE, agents, models, and ops working as one product. Proposed only, not a committed release schedule. No dates. No SWE percentages. No “beats” claims.

Ordered priorities

  • 1 — Coding agent quality — Prove the agent with scored, reproducible evaluations before expanding the feature surface.
  • 2 — Developer experience — Indexing, parallel agents, diffs, autocomplete, remote polish.
  • 3 — Model infrastructure — Routing, hardware-aware scheduling, local-first hybrid options, usage insight.
  • 4 — Team readiness — Access control, audit logging, recovery, reproducible deploys (substantial new work).
  • 5 — Ecosystem — Plugins, MCP, community packs, forge integrations, public roadmap governance.

Focus when resources are limited

  • Autonomous verified development (tests + human approval, not just codegen)
  • Intelligent local model and GPU orchestration
  • Persistent project intelligence
  • Isolated multi-agent worktrees
  • Self-host administration you can trust

Architecture stance

Keep IDE, Agents, and Platform modular with clear APIs. Support optional agent backends while the Spockify experience stays consistent. Default loop is one kernel: runHarness.

Git intelligence

Treat AI Git collaboration as a first-class capability: intelligent commits, branches, PRs, and review — with human approval and transparent AI attribution. Agents must not silently impersonate human authors.

Honesty bar

  • Do not claim superiority until evaluation gates are closed with full reproducibility.
  • Single-tenant operator production is not enterprise or shared SaaS.
  • Signed SBOM exists; independent audit and legal sign-off do not.
